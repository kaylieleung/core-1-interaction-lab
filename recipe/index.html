02step 1

first,

Collect.

1Rummage through trash. It could be yours or someone else’s. (Make sure it’s clean.)

2Assemble a pile of different trash ephemera. Be sure to collect a variety of textures, colors, opacities. The more range, the more visually interesting your composition becomes.

Examples

of materiality

all sorts of trash!

carton

wrappers

paper

paper towel

ziploc

plastic

cardstock

pearls

03step 2

next,

Cut.

1Using only found material, hole punch items at least twice.

2The amount of circles created depends on instinct, amount of material, ease of hole punching, texture/color, etc.

3If there are materials that are the same size as the hole-punched circles (example: I had pearl stickers), you can also include them.

4After hole punching, gather the circles into a container.

Examples

of hole punching

just keep cutting until you get...
a collection of circles!
04step 3

last,

Collage.

1At this point, you can continue to add more material into your collection. The total amount you accumulate is up to you.

2Arrange the circles into a large grid by choosing circles from the cup at random. Adhere them down to a surface (could be a board, piece of paper, etc.).

3Repeat the above steps until you’ve created a grid you’re satisfied with.

Examples

of compositions

the grid can be small...
or large!
